##  About this blog

I've never been a very strong writer. I've always brushed it off as "well, I'm more of math person". So what if I suck at writing? Who cares if I don't write good?

Turns out I care. Also, turns out mathematicians care. In fact, turns out a lot of the job consists in writing and reading what others have written. More than anything, turns out it'd be nice if I could write better.

This blog is an attempt to get better at writing. I'm open to feedback, so let me know what you think!
