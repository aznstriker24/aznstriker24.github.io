## This is a level two header

Let $$\zeta(s)$$ denote the Riemann zeta function.
For $$ \mathfrak{Re}(s) > 1 $$, it is defined as

$$ \zeta(s) = \sum_{n = 1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^s}. $$

Doing some clever rearranging, we find that

$$ \zeta(s) = \prod_p \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}} $$

where the product ranges over all prime numbers $$p$$.
The factors

$$ L_p(s) = \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}} $$

in the product is the *$$p$$-adic $$L$$-factor*.
Interestingly, for positive integers $$n$$, one finds that $$L_p(n)$$ is related to the volume of the $$p$$-adic unit $$(n-1)$$-spheres.

##  Another header at level two

Consider the gamma function $$\Gamma(z)$$, defined for $$\mathfrak{Re}(z) > 1$$ as

$$ \Gamma(z) = \int_0^\infty t^z e^t \, \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t}. $$

and then analytic-continued to a meromorphic function on $$\mathbb{C}$$.
It is characterized by the property that $$\Gamma(s+1) = s \Gamma(s)$$.
In particular, we have $$\Gamma(n) = (n-1)!$$ for every natural numer $$n$$.

Our current interest in the gamma function is in defining the *archimedean $$L$$-factor*

$$ L_\infty(s) = \pi^{-s/2} \Gamma(s/2). $$

In some sense, this is meant to be an "archimedean analogue" of the $$p$$-adic $$L$$-factor.
Amazingly, the value of $$L_\infty(s)$$ at a positive integers $$n$$ is also related to volume of (ordinary) unite $$(n-1)$$-spheres

Now let

$$ Z(s) = \pi^{-s/2} \Gamma(s/2 ) \zeta(s) = \prod_v L_v(s) $$

where the product ranges over all *places*, *i.e.* all prime numbers $$p$$ as well as the "infinite prime" $$\infty$$.

> **Theorem.**
> $$Z(1-s) = Z(s)$$.
